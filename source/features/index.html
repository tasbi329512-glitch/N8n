Debug & monitor

Every run leaves a trail

Immutable execution logs with per-step inputs, outputs, and timings. Re-run from the failure, test with mock data, and roll back with version history.

  • Step-level replay with editable mock data
  • Error workflows that catch failures and alert your way
  • Metrics and log streaming to your observability stack

Trigger payload, every node's input and output, timing per step, AI prompts and tool calls, and the final status. Logs are immutable and retained per your plan.

Yes — sandbox mode runs AI steps against mock data, and test executions never count toward your quota or fire real side effects.

Attach an error workflow to any automation: on failure it receives the full execution context and can page, ticket, or auto-remediate however you define.
