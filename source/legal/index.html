Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run Flowmint: your account details, workspace configuration, and execution metadata. We never sell personal data, and workflow payloads are processed only to execute your automations.

You can export or delete your data at any time from workspace settings. Analytics on this marketing site are privacy-friendly and cookie-free; the app itself uses strictly necessary cookies for sign-in only.

Security

Flowmint Cloud runs on SOC 2-audited infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest. Credentials are stored in a dedicated vault, never in workflow definitions or logs.

Found a vulnerability? Please report it via our contact form — we triage security reports within 24 hours and credit researchers with permission.

License

Flowmint is fair-code: the Community Edition is free to self-host for most uses. You may run it internally, modify it, and use it commercially — you may not offer Flowmint itself as a competing hosted service. Paid plans add Cloud hosting, collaboration, and enterprise controls.

Community templates and nodes carry their own licenses, shown on each listing.
